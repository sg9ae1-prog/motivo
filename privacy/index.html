Motivo

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

The short version

  • Your goals, tasks and chats live on your phone, not on our server.
  • Messages to Motivo pass through our server and are never stored there.
  • The AI companies that answer may not use them to train AI or keep them for themselves.
  • On our server we keep your account: your email, your name and a few settings.
  • We don’t sell your data and there are no ads. You can delete your account any time.

Who we are

Motivo is made by Magpy, a company registered in Israel. When this page says “we” or “us”, it means Magpy.

Questions about your data? Write to contact@magpy.co. A real person reads it.

Signing in

You sign in with Google, or with Apple on an iPhone. There is no password to make or forget. You need to sign in to use Motivo.

Google or Apple tells us your email address and your name. If you use Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we only get the hidden address Apple gives us.

What stays on your phone

Your goals, tasks, habits, streaks, chats with Motivo, what Motivo remembers about you, and how you set Motivo up. All of this is saved only on your phone.

That means a new phone starts empty, even if you sign in with the same account. It also means that if you delete the app, this is gone.

What we keep on our server

Our server runs on Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany (in the EU). For each account it keeps:

  • your email address and name
  • whether you signed in with Google or Apple
  • whether you said yes to news emails from us, and when
  • whether your phone is an iPhone or Android, and which version of Motivo it runs
  • when you first used Motivo, and the last day you used it
  • whether you have Motivo Pro
  • how many messages and searches you used today, so the free limits work

None of this includes what you write to Motivo.

Talking to Motivo (the AI)

When you chat with Motivo, or ask it to plan a goal, your phone sends the message to our server. The message includes what you wrote and what Motivo needs to answer well, like the name you gave Motivo, your goals and your recent tasks.

Our server passes it to OpenRouter. OpenRouter hands it to an AI company, and the answer comes back the same way.

  • Your messages are never stored on our server. We don’t log them either.
  • We tell OpenRouter to use only AI companies that promise your messages are never used to train AI and are not kept for their own use. Some may hold them briefly for safety checks.
  • OpenRouter and the AI companies don’t get your email or your account details from us.

Voice

When you talk to Motivo out loud, your phone’s own speech service turns your voice into text. That is Apple’s on an iPhone and Google’s on Android. They may send the sound to their servers to do this, under their own privacy rules. Motivo only gets the text, and treats it like a typed message.

When Motivo talks back, it uses your phone’s built-in voice.

Guide videos from YouTube

Some tasks come with a video. To find one, our server sends a short search, like “beginner guitar chords”, to YouTube. YouTube sees our server, not you.

We save the results for 7 days so the next person with the same search gets them faster. We store a scrambled code of the search, not the words, and nothing that says who searched.

When you watch a video, you are watching YouTube. Google’s privacy policy and YouTube’s terms apply to that.

Paying for Pro

You pay through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card.

RevenueCat is the company that tells us whether you have Pro. It knows your account number with us and your purchases, but not your name or email. We keep a copy of your Pro status on our server so the app can check it quickly.

When something breaks

If the app crashes or hits an error, a report goes to Sentry, a service that helps us fix bugs. The report says what went wrong, which screens you visited just before, your phone model and system, the app version, and your account number.

It does not include your email, your name, your IP address, or anything you wrote.

Notifications

Motivo reminds you about your tasks with notifications. The app sets them up on your phone. We don’t send them from a server. You can turn them off in your phone’s settings, or under Activity reminders in Motivo’s Settings.

Early sign-ups on this website

If you join the early sign-up list on this website, we keep your email address and the time you signed up. It is stored on the same server in Frankfurt.

We use it only to tell you when Motivo launches. To be taken off the list, email contact@magpy.co.

News emails

When you sign in, there is a box to say yes to news emails from us. It starts unticked. We only ever email people who ticked it. You can change your answer in Settings at any time.

Companies outside the EU

Some of the companies named on this page are outside the EU, including in the United States: RevenueCat, Sentry, OpenRouter and the AI companies, Google (and YouTube) and Apple. Each one handles your data under its own privacy policy.

What we never do

  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use a tracking or analytics service.

Age

Motivo is for people aged 13 and over. If you live in the EU and are under 16, a parent or guardian needs to agree before you use Motivo.

If you think someone under 13 is using Motivo, email us and we will delete their account.

How long we keep things

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Today’s message and search counts: cleared after 48 hours.
  • Saved video search results: cleared after 7 days.
  • Your messages: never stored on our server.
  • Crash reports: only as long as we need them to fix problems.
  • Early sign-up email: until you ask us to remove it.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we have about you
  • fix anything that is wrong
  • delete your data
  • give you a copy of it
  • stop using it for something you didn’t agree to

Email contact@magpy.co and we will answer within 30 days.

You can delete your account yourself in the app: Settings → Delete account. Here is how. Your Apple or Google subscription is not cancelled by this. You cancel it in the store.

If you live in the EU, the GDPR (Europe’s privacy law) protects you. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the data-protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will put the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
