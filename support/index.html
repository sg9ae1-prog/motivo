Email contact@magpy.co. Tell us what happened and which phone you use, and we’ll get back to you.

I paid for Pro, but the app doesn’t show it

Use restore purchases:

  1. Make sure you are signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used before.
  2. Open Settings in Motivo and tap See Motivo Pro.
  3. At the bottom, tap Restore purchases.

Pro is linked to your Motivo account, so it also follows you to a new phone, even from iPhone to Android.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You cancel in the store you bought it from, not in Motivo.

  • iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Motivo.
  • Android: open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Motivo.

You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I can’t sign in

  • You can sign in with Google on any phone, and with Apple on an iPhone.
  • Check that you are online. Signing in needs the internet.
  • If the Google or Apple window closes before it finishes, try again.
  • Still stuck? Email us and tell us which phone you use and which button you tapped.

I got a new phone and my goals are gone

Your goals, tasks and chats are saved only on the phone you made them on. That keeps them private, but it also means they don’t move to a new phone. Your account and Pro do move: sign in with the same Google or Apple account.

Motivo says I’ve run out of messages

The free version has a small number of chat messages a day. They come back tomorrow. Pro has unlimited chat. Your habits, tasks and streak always keep working.

I’m not getting reminders

Check that notifications for Motivo are turned on in your phone’s settings, and look at Activity reminders in Motivo’s Settings.

How do I delete my account?

In Motivo, open Settings and tap Delete account. More about deleting your account.
