Motivo

Delete your account

Last updated: 23 October 2026

You can delete your Motivo account at any time. You don’t need to give a reason.

In the app

  1. Open Motivo and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account again to confirm.

It happens straight away. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to disconnect Motivo from your Apple ID.

Without the app

Email contact@magpy.co from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We may write back to check it’s really you. We delete it within 30 days, and tell you when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and everything on our server about you: email, name, sign-in method, your answer about news emails, phone type, app version and dates.
  • Your Pro status and today’s message counts.
  • When you delete in the app: your goals, tasks, chats and memories on that phone. If you delete by email, delete the app too, because those are only on your phone and we can’t reach them.

What isn’t deleted

  • Your subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play, or you may still be charged. Here is how.
  • Purchase records. Apple or Google keep a record of what you bought, and so does RevenueCat, the company that tells us who has Pro. To have RevenueCat’s record deleted too, email us.
  • Crash reports. These carry an account number but nothing you wrote, and are kept only as long as we need them to fix problems.

If you joined the early sign-up list on this website, email us to be taken off it too.
