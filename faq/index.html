Motivo

Questions and answers

What is Motivo?

Motivo is a mobile app with a friendly AI companion that helps you reach any goal, such as learning a skill, building a habit or finishing a project. It turns your goal into small daily steps, reminds you when it is time, and adapts when you miss a day.

Who is Motivo for?

Anyone with something they want to get done and a hard time keeping going. The goal can be anything: learning guitar, a daily walk, a side project or a calmer evening.

Is Motivo free?

Yes. Habits, tasks, streaks, a morning hello and a little chat are free to use. An optional Pro subscription adds unlimited chat, voice, memory, personalities and task guides, with a free trial first. Prices are shown in the app store. See the terms of use.

Is my data private?

Your goals, tasks, habits and chats live on your phone. Messages to Motivo pass through our server and are not stored there. The AI companies that answer have promised not to train AI on them. Motivo does not sell your data and shows no ads. Read the privacy policy.

Does Motivo use AI?

Yes. When you chat with Motivo or ask it to plan a goal, your message goes to an AI model that writes the reply. Motivo uses AI companies that promise not to train on your messages or keep them for their own use.

When does Motivo launch?

Motivo is coming soon and is not in the app stores yet. You can join the early-access list on the home page to hear the day it launches.

Does Motivo work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, Motivo is being made for both iPhone and Android. You sign in with Google on either phone, and with Apple on an iPhone.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account in the app, or by email. The delete-account page explains the steps and what gets deleted.
